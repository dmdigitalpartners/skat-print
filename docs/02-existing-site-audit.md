## Product Categories (5 confirmed)

### 1. Shelves, Stands & Stoppers (Стелажи, щендери, стопери)
- Retail display solutions, corrugated and laminated cardboard
- Known internally as "мълчаливия продавач" (the silent seller) of the brand
- Custom-branded, designed exactly per client spec
- Do not consume existing retail shelf space
- 14+ gallery examples on old site

### 2. Wine Boxes & BiB (Кутии за вино, BiB)
- Bag-in-Box (BiB) laminated corrugated packaging
- Primary: alcoholic beverages (wine, spirits, beer)
- Secondary: non-alcoholic beverages
- Die-cut openings, luxury finishes, custom shapes
- "meet the requirements for strength and quality"

### 3. Food Packaging (Опаковки за хранителната промишленост)
- Cardboard and laminated corrugated
- "eye-catching design and attractive shapes"
- Protects fragile items (cupcakes, biscuits, delicate foods)
- Must meet food industry quality and safety standards
- Notable copy: "no one likes a crushed cupcake or broken biscuits, despite the preserved taste qualities"

### 4. Cosmetics & Perfumery Boxes (Опаковки за парфюмерия и козметика)
- Luxury boxes from cardboard, microcorrugated, laminated cardboard
- Advanced post-printing technologies
- "packaging plays a key role in product selection"
- Serves as "direct communication between the user and your product"
- Various colors, shapes, sizes

### 5. Custom & Gift Packaging (Нестандартни и подаръчни опаковки)
- Non-standard sizing, individual specifications
- Exposed corrugated outer layer aesthetic
- Microcorrugated and laminated materials
- Post-printing technologies
- Enables brand showcase and new marketing strategies

## Services / Manufacturing Capabilities

### Printing
- **Offset printing** via two presses:
  - Roland: 52 × 74 cm sheet format
  - Heidelberg: 71 × 102 cm sheet format

### Corrugated Board Production (own production line)
- "N" wave: 1 mm thickness
- "E" wave (microflute): 1.5 mm thickness
- "B" wave: 3 mm thickness
- Five-layer corrugated (B + E profiles)
- Moisture-resistant corrugated variants
- Two-layer corrugated with cardboard covering up to 103 cm width

### Finishing
- Laminating: matte, gloss, silver foil, gold foil
- Die-stamping / die-cutting: unlimited sizes
- Embossing (for luxury/cosmetics packaging)
